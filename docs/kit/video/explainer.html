QA Agent Kit

Test any project with a team of AI testers.

Here is how it works, in about two minutes.

Step 1 · Get the kit

Clone it, install it, open the setup screen.

$ git clone https://github.com/Alirizvi987/qa-agent-kit $ cd qa-agent-kit $ npm install && npx playwright install chromium $ npm run setup Open this address in your browser: 127.0.0.1:4321
Step 2 · Fill in the form · 1 of 7

Name the project. Say what it does.

Two or three plain sentences. The agents read this first.

The orange ring shows what to fill in
Step 2 · Fill in the form · 2 of 7

List where it runs. Tick the live one.

Agents only ever look at the live product. They never change it.

Step 2 · Fill in the form · 3 of 7

Add each website, admin panel or API.

Pick what it is and how people sign in.

Step 2 · Fill in the form · 4 of 7

Add every type of user.

Each role gets its own AI tester. Tick which one is staff.

Step 2 · Fill in the form · 5 of 7

Add every state an account can be in.

Type one and press Add. Every change is checked against all of them.

Step 2 · Fill in the form · 6 of 7

Got an app? Tick it.

Android package or iPhone bundle id. No app? Skip this step.

Step 2 · Fill in the form · 7 of 7

Press Create. Your workspace is ready.

Rules, agents and checklists, written for your product.
